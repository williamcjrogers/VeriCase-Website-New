## Product and audience

VeriCase helps construction lawyers, claims consultants and contractors examine email and document evidence, establish chronology, research cited answers, address opposing arguments and prepare claims. The website explains those capabilities and invites an email enquiry. It is not the working application or an interactive software demonstration.

## Brand and type

Use the supplied, real VeriCase wordmark. Forest green #0B2516, deep green #041A0F, parchment #F5F0E6, paper #FCFAF5, brass #BF9B58, dark bronze #5C431B. Existing secondary text #535A6E and rule #D8CDB6 remain available. Brass is an accent, not body text on paper. No blue brand, gradients, ornamental tech imagery, or new palette.

Newsreader 500 for headings; IBM Plex Sans 400/500/600 for body and controls. Sentence case, one-colour headings, body measure at most 68ch, left aligned. Body 17px with 1.65 line height, lead 18px to 21px, h3 22px to 28px, h2 32px to 52px. Hero uses the existing clarity scale, 42px to 64px, 1.06 line height. Preserve the existing wordmark and navigation.

## Layout and interaction

Desktop container max-width 1280px including 40px side padding. Mobile side padding 16px, increasing to 24px at 640px. Existing breakpoints 640, 768, 1024, 1280, 1440px. Section rhythm 44px to 88px. Buttons have at least 44px targets, 2px corners and visible 3px focus. Dark green bands separate substantial topics from parchment reading areas; do not add a card around every paragraph.

Keep the reading order: approved opening with evidence example; full approved time section; capabilities with substantive descriptions; shared workspace and intended users; four full biographies; FAQ; enquiry. Restore existing detailed copy, rather than invent shorter substitutes. The short three-job overview can lead to the fuller sections.

Base product illustrations on the actual application's screenshots. Preserve the real layout, labels, hierarchy and relationships. Focus or enlarge one useful part of a screen rather than inventing a simplified replacement interface. A readable document associated with its supporting statement is appropriate only when grounded in those references. User-triggered interaction may reveal the source or change a clearly labelled view where this reflects the application. Before any click, readers must understand the example. One obvious action produces one obvious result. No autoplay, decorative scans, counters or networks. Full capability explanations and biographies must remain visible without interaction. Native disclosures are appropriate for credentials and reported matters.
