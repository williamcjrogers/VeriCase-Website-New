# VeriCase Visual Branding Landscape Brief: eDiscovery, Legal AI and Construction Claims (as at 27 September 2026)

My recommendation is that VeriCase build its identity on a **"Datum & Register"** system rather than on "truth" or "ledger" imagery. The system has three parts. The first is a graduated chronology spine with a surveyor's-benchmark mark at "now". The second is sequential register numbering in the style of court bundles. The third is a single seal-red colour used only when a record is cryptographically sealed. This matters because the words in the proposed positioning are already taken by incumbents: Everlaw and Relativity own "truth", Oracle Aconex owns "unalterable audit trail" and "single source of truth", and Harvey and Legora own "precision".\[1\]\[2\]\[3\] What nobody in the set uses is time itself as the organising visual idea.

## TL;DR

- **Recommended direction:** Build a "Datum & Register" system.
  - The signature asset is a ruled chronology spine with ISO 8601 ticks and a benchmark cut-mark. It should work as logo, UI and layout grid.
  - Colour: white-bond and graphite as the base, registrar blue-black for record text, and one vermilion seal accent reserved for verified states.
  - Type: a grotesk with a monospace companion and tabular figures. No editorial serif.
- **Clichés to avoid:**
  - legal AI's warm-cream-plus-high-contrast-serif "premium law firm lobby" look (Harvey, Legora, and Everlaw's 2026 parchment site);
  - Opus 2-style navy/purple/turquoise gradients;
  - construction hi-vis orange and site photography;
  - anything block, chain or hexagon (the "ledger" trap);
  - scales, gavels and navy-and-gold.
- **Positioning pushback:**
  - "Immutable Ledger of Truth" overclaims three times: tribunals find facts; hash-chaining gives tamper-evidence, not immutability; "ledger" reads crypto.
  - "Precision Instrument" is already used by Harvey and Legora, and there is an actual Emerson "Rosemount VeriCase" precision calibration instrument.
  - Replace with something like **"The contemporaneous record, fixed."** — anchored to the SCL Protocol's language and backed by RFC 3161 or qualified timestamps.

## 1. Executive summary

**The one recommended direction: "Datum & Register"**

*Metaphor system*
- **Primary: the surveyor's datum or benchmark.** It is a fixed reference that everything else is measured from.
  - It is construction-native: Ordnance Survey cut marks, site datum levels, "true" and "plumb".
  - It says "exactness" without saying "luxury", because a benchmark is a chiselled mark on a public wall, not a Swiss watch.
- **Secondary: the bound register.** Numbered, sequential, append-only entries, like bundle pagination (e.g. `VC-000417`), exhibit labels and drawing revision tables.
- **The synthesis is the "spine".**
  - A ruled time axis with graduated ticks, a benchmark mark at the reference point, and register entries hung from it in order.
  - The same object works as logo (the mark cropped from the spine), UI (the chronology view itself) and layout grid (every marketing page hangs off a ruled baseline).

*Palette logic*
- Base: true white bond and graphite. Deliberately not cream, which legal AI now occupies.
- Record text: registrar blue-black.
- Verification: one vermilion "seal" accent, shown only when a record has been hashed and timestamped.
- Time: shown by position on the spine, never by a rainbow.

*Typography direction*
- A sober grotesk with a true monospace companion, tabular lining figures, a slashed zero and unambiguous Il1/0O.
- ISO 8601 timestamps and short hashes are the display typography.
- No high-contrast editorial serif.

*Avoid*
1. The 2025–26 legal-AI "law firm lobby" look: warm off-white or near-black, a refined serif, cinematic photography.
2. Gradients in navy, purple or teal, the AI sparkle and the logo wall.
3. Block, chain, hexagon or neon-on-black imagery, which becomes fatal once you use the word "ledger".
4. Hi-vis orange, hard hats, cranes, blueprint blue and BIM renders.
5. Scales, gavels, columns, marble, navy and gold.

**Rejected alternatives (two only)**
- **Stratigraphy / ice core.** It is beautiful and chronological, but strata get folded, eroded and overturned. That is the opposite of the evidential claim. It also collapses into the generic "layered data" SaaS illustration.
- **Seal / letterpress impression as the master idea.** It is excellent for verification but drifts into heritage, luxury and legal tradition, which re-creates the premium you want to remove. It survives only as the vermilion seal state.

## 6. Positioning pushback

**"Immutable Ledger" reads crypto.**
- Yes. "Immutable" and "Ledger" are both well-known crypto company names, and "immutable ledger" is the textbook definition of a blockchain.
- For construction clients (contractors, QSs, employers) the association is more likely to be doubt than admiration.
- Drop "ledger" from customer-facing language. Keep it, if at all, as internal architecture vocabulary.

**"Truth" overclaims.**
- It overclaims to exactly the audience you sell to. Adjudicators, arbitrators and TCC judges find facts. A platform that claims "truth" invites a cross-examination line ("your software told you it was true?").
- It is also not ownable: Everlaw ("illuminating truth", "a straighter path to the truth") and Relativity ("discover the truth") already hold it.\[1\]\[11\]\[16\]
- The defensible vocabulary is **contemporaneous, provenance, integrity, tamper-evident, chain of custody, fixed at a verifiable time**.
- It also ties directly to the SCL Delay and Disruption Protocol (2nd ed., February 2017), whose Core Principle 1 states that "Contracting parties should reach a clear agreement on the type of records to be kept and allocate the necessary resources to meet that agreement", and whose Core Principle 4 is headed "Do not 'wait and see' regarding impact of delay events (contemporaneous analysis)" — a standard your buyers already use.

**"Ledger of Truth" echoes "single source of truth".**
- Yes, and Aconex already uses that exact SaaS phrase.\[34\] It would make VeriCase sound like a CDE, not a dispute-evidence tool.

**Is "immutable" technically defensible?**
- Mostly no. Cloud records are not physically immutable.
- What you can defend:
  - **Tamper-evidence:** SHA-256 hashes per item, hash-chained per matter, so any change is detectable.
  - **Time-fixing:** RFC 3161 timestamp tokens from an external timestamp authority. For stronger evidential weight, UK/EU qualified electronic timestamps from a qualified trust service provider: under eIDAS Art. 41(2), retained in the UK version, "A qualified electronic time stamp shall enjoy the presumption of the accuracy of the date and the time it indicates and the integrity of the data to which the date and time are bound."
  - **Retention control:** WORM/object-lock storage.
- Every visual claim needs a product counterpart:
  - The **vermilion seal** state may only render when a hash and timestamp token exist and verify.
  - The **short-hash** shown in the UI must be clickable to a verification receipt.
  - The **benchmark mark at "now"** implies an external time source. Use one.
  - Don't show "sealed" on ingested items whose pre-ingestion provenance is unknown. Show "fixed on receipt by VeriCase" instead. VeriCase fixes when it received something; it cannot vouch for the document before that.

**"Veri-" crowding and existing VeriCase brands.**
- The prefix is heavily used: Veritas (itself in eDiscovery and information governance), Verisign, Verisk, Veritone, Veriff, Verint.
- Specific "VeriCase" hits:
  - **VERICASE LTD**, Companies House no. 16562435, incorporated 6 July 2025, London W1W; SIC codes 62012, 62020, 71129.\[54\] This may be your own company; confirm.
  - **veri-case.com**, titled "VeriCase – Dispute Intelligence Platform", with "Evidence provenance" and "ISO 27001 controls" on its login page.\[55\] This may also be yours.
  - **vericase.com**, a separate login-only "VeriCase" service ("use the authorization link previously provided").\[56\] Owner unknown.
  - A **LinkedIn "Vericase"** company page.\[57\]
  - **Emerson's Rosemount VeriCase**, a calibration and verification case for radar level transmitters with "Accuracy: Within ±1mm".\[58\] A literal precision instrument with your name.
- Implication:
  - Commission UKIPO/EUIPO clearance in classes 9, 42 and 45 before any identity spend.
  - The name gives almost no distinctiveness by itself, so the visual system must carry recognition. That is another reason to invest in a shape asset (the spine) rather than a colour.

**"Precision Instrument" vs "eliminate the legacy premium".**
- There is a real conflict. Precision-instrument branding (Leica, Braun, brass chronometers) signals price.
- "Precision" is also already Harvey's and Legora's word.\[2\]\[3\]
- Signal exactness through **public, working-grade standards**: a tape measure, the OS benchmark, ISO 8601, bundle pagination, a spirit level. These are tools everybody owns, and they are exact because they are standard, not because they are expensive.
- Say "instrument of record" rather than "precision instrument". Publish pricing, as Everlaw's "all-inclusive pricing" precedent shows.\[1\]

**Proposed wording (one recommendation)**
- Master line: **"The contemporaneous record, fixed."**
- Descriptor: *"A pre-dispute chronology workspace. Every item hashed, timestamped and in sequence."*
- It claims only what the product can prove, uses the Protocol's own word, and leaves fact-finding to the tribunal.

## 8. Colour

### 8a. What the research actually supports

- **Labrecque & Milne (2012), *Journal of the Academy of Marketing Science* 40:711–727.**
  - Across four studies, hue maps onto brand-personality dimensions (blue with competence, red with excitement), and saturation and value amplify these effects.\[59\]\[60\]
  - This is lab evidence with generic stimuli. It supports "blue-family reads competent", which is why the whole category is blue or navy. That is a reason for VeriCase to use blue-black only as a functional record colour, not as a differentiator.
- **Elliot & Maier (2012 color-in-context theory, *Advances in Experimental Social Psychology* 45:63–125; 2014 review, *Annual Review of Psychology* 65:95–120).**
  - Colour meaning is context-dependent: red can signal danger or failure in achievement contexts, and approach or relevance elsewhere.\[61\]
  - Implication for VeriCase: a red accent only means "verified / sealed" if the system teaches that meaning consistently. Used loosely, red in a dispute context will read as "error" or "adverse".
- **Candour on the evidence.**
  - Much popular colour psychology (e.g. "blue = trust" charts) is folk wisdom.\[62\] The academic effects are modest, context-bound and often from small student samples.
  - Research on colour as a website trustworthiness cue exists but does not produce transferable rules.\[63\]
- **Distinctiveness evidence outweighs it.**
  - Romaniuk's Ehrenberg-Bass framework measures brand assets on **fame** and **uniqueness**.\[64\]
  - A 2026 benchmarking study in *International Journal of Advertising*, "Shape-based assets are strongest: benchmarking distinctive brand asset performance across industries" (published online 5 March 2026), which analysed 1,162 distinctive assets across 21 categories, four countries and nine years, found shape-based assets strongest (40% fame, 71% uniqueness) and colour assets weakest (12% fame, 39% uniqueness).
  - Conclusion: **the spine and benchmark mark (shape) should carry recognition; colour should do functional work.**

### 8b. Candidate directions evaluated

| Direction | Verdict | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Black-and-white documentary monochrome ("in black and white") | **Adopt as base** | Idiomatically means "on the record"; cheapest to reproduce; unoccupied in cool form (Harvey's monochrome is warm) |
| Archival blue-black ink | **Adopt for record text** | Statutory-register association; must be distinguishably darker and greyer than navy to avoid the legal cliché |
| Single vermilion / seal red | **Adopt as the only accent**, with strict meaning | Seal association; context-taught (Elliot & Maier). Must stay distinct from DISCO coral #FF5859 and Relativity orange: use deeper, more yellow-red vermilion\[19\] |
| Archival paper / buff tones | **Reject as base** | Now occupied: Everlaw #F1F0EA, Legora #F3F2EE/#FAFAF9, Harvey warm off-white\[1\]\[29\] |
| Brass / bronze of standards | Reject | Luxury; conflicts with anti-premium |
| Cyanotype / blueprint | Reject | Construction cliché |
| Diazo / sepia | Reject | Nostalgic; reads as aged, not fixed |
| Hi-vis orange | Reject | Construction cliché; also Relativity's colour |
| Navy and gold | Reject | Legal cliché; Opus 2 already uses #051736 plus #EDB043\[32\] |
| Purple gradients | Reject | AI/SaaS cliché; Opus 2 uses #641DAB→#180767\[32\] |
| Neon on black | Reject | Crypto/cyber cliché; fatal with "ledger" |

### 8c. Proposed palette logic by role

These are starting values chosen by the author. The contrast ratios are the author's calculations and must be re-checked with a contrast tool.

| Role | Proposed value | Use | Contrast vs #FFFFFF |
|---|---|---|---|
| Base – surface | #FFFFFF (white bond) | Record surfaces, marketing ground | — |
| Base – ink | #1A1A1A (graphite) | UI text, rules, logo | ≈17:1 |
| Record | #1F2A44 (registrar blue-black) | Text of record entries, quoted evidence | ≈14:1 |
| Rule / tick | #8A8F98 (survey grey) | Spine graduations, dividers (non-text) | ≈3.2:1 (meets WCAG 1.4.11's 3:1 for non-text) |
| Verification accent | #C8341C (vermilion seal) | *Only* the sealed/verified state and the benchmark mark at "now"; aim for ≤2% of any surface | ≈5.3:1 (passes 4.5:1 for text) |
| Time / sequence | No hue | Position on the spine plus ISO 8601 labels; density shown with graphite tints (e.g. 100/60/30%) | Text labels stay graphite |

**Functional colour rules for the chronology UI**
- Encode **time by position**, never by colour.
- Encode **state** by shape, then label, then colour:
  - ● filled vermilion = sealed and verified;
  - ○ outline graphite = received, not yet sealed;
  - ◐ = disputed or annotated;
  - strikethrough-free "superseded" with a revision letter, borrowed from title-block convention (a superseded record stays visible and gets a revision letter; nothing is struck out).
- For **parties** (employer, contractor, subcontractors, consultants), use a colour-blind-safe categorical set such as Okabe–Ito, always paired with a text label or initials.
- Never rely on red versus green. Test with deuteranopia and protanopia simulation.
- Meet WCAG 2.2: 4.5:1 for body text, 3:1 for large text and non-text UI components.

## 9. Typography and UI implications

**Overused in this set (observed)**
- High-contrast editorial serifs: Harvey's TWK Ghost, and Legora's "refined serif" (reported as Playfair [3P]).\[24\]\[31\]
- The Dinamo grotesk look: ABC Diatype at Harvey.\[24\]
- The Vercel-ecosystem monospace: Geist Mono, reported at Legora [3P].\[31\]
- Geometric friendly sans: Museo Sans in Relativity's older wordmark.\[11\]
- Inter and Söhne are widely reported across AI/SaaS but were not confirmed for these eight brands. Avoid them anyway as generic.

**Direction**
- One grotesk family with a **true monospace companion**. Must-haves:
  - tabular lining figures (for column-aligned dates and amounts);
  - slashed or dotted zero;
  - distinct Il1;
  - good small-size legibility.
- The **display type is data**: `2026-09-27T14:03:11Z`, `VC-000417`, `sha256:9f2c…e41a`.
- Candidate families to license-test, in order of preference:
  - GT Standard with GT Standard Mono (Grilli Type);
  - Atlas Grotesk with Atlas Typewriter (Commercial Type);
  - Maison Neue with Maison Neue Mono (Milieu Grotesque).
- Rule out Geist, Inter, Diatype and any high-contrast serif.
- A lettering reference worth giving the designer is ISO 3098 technical-drawing lettering. It is construction-native, unfashionable and exact.

**ISO 8601 as a motif**
- Use full ISO dates everywhere, in UI and marketing. They sort chronologically, avoid UK/US ambiguity (03/04), and look like evidence rather than decoration.
- Pair each with a UK-readable secondary format on hover or print ("27 Sep 2026, 15:03 BST"). Tribunals read documents, not ISO strings.

**The spine as signature asset**
- **Logo:** a short segment of the spine, with graduations and the benchmark cut-mark (⊥ with chevron) in vermilion at the reference tick.
- **Product:** the chronology view is literally that spine.
  - Entries hang from ticks.
  - Density is shown by tick weight.
  - Sealed entries carry the vermilion dot.
  - The metadata panel is a drawing title block (ID, source system such as Aconex or Outlook, revision, status code, hash, timestamp token).
- **Marketing:**
  - Every page sits on a ruled baseline with a running timestamp in the margin.
  - Section numbers are register numbers.
  - Case studies are laid out as bundle extracts with tab dividers.
- **Motion:** a mechanical "tick", like the step of a counter, as entries are fixed in place. No easing flourishes, glow or particle effects.
- **Print and PDF export:** bundle-ready pagination and a verification footer carrying the hash and timestamp token on every page. This is where the brand proves itself before a tribunal.
