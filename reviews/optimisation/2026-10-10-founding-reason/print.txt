THE REASON WE BUILT VERICASE



Your case begins
with the record.
As Max W. Abrahamson observed in Engineering Law and the I.C.E. Contracts (first
published in 1965):


   “A party to a dispute, particularly if there is arbitration, will learn
   three lessons (often too late): the importance of records, the
   importance of records and the importance of records. It is
   impossible to exaggerate the extent to which lawyers can find
   unexpected grounds, often quite real, on which to cast doubt
   on evidence if it is not backed by meticulously established
   records.” [Emphasis added]

VeriCase exists so these lessons need not be learned the
hard way.


Time is your ally. Not your enemy.
Time is what every practice is trying to keep. The advantage lies with the
people who can see what the record shows before the timetable runs out.

VeriCase turns a disordered project record into arguments that can be
checked against their sources. It is for the people who prepare claims, and
for the lawyers and consultants who work with them.
It is most useful at the start of a matter. A factual chronology used to mean
reading tens of thousands of emails by hand. VeriCase puts that
correspondence in order, and each entry keeps its source.

The firms that work this way advise from the record. The others are still

VeriCase is for working with the project record: searching it, asking it
questions, discussing it and building bundles. It works alongside your
existing document systems.



  What can we work with?

Mailboxes (PST), individual emails (EML and MSG), PDFs, Word documents,
spreadsheets and images. Text recognition helps make scanned pages
searchable. We can discuss your record types and preparation needs
during a demonstration.

Admissibility and weight are for the tribunal. Review the underlying
records, the source references and the final document with the

Every answer shows its sources, so you can read them yourself. Review the
