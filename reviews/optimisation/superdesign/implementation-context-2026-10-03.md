## Section chronology-lens
Layout classes: `clarity-section capability-explanation bg-paper`

p: Preparation and chronology

h2: Put the project record in order.

p: Bring project emails, attachments and documents into a record your team can work through. Examine correspondence in date order and return to the source when a date, instruction or account needs checking.

p: A delivery forecast, an instruction and a confirmation can sit in different mailboxes. Reading them together helps the team distinguish what was expected from what the record says happened.

p: Threading and quoted-text handling help make correspondence easier to follow. Duplicate handling and relevance controls reduce repeated material in the review view; your team checks the selection.

h3: Bring the records together

p: Work with email archives and individual messages, their attachments, PDFs, Word documents, spreadsheets and images. Text recognition helps make scanned pages searchable.

h3: Follow the correspondence

p: Read messages in context, with threading and quoted-text handling to help distinguish a new reply from earlier correspondence.

h3: Review repeated material

p: Duplicate handling can set repeated messages aside. Near-duplicate checks cover certain similar messages; review exclusions when assessing the record.

h3: Focus on the matter

p: Use dates, search terms and relevance controls to narrow the records under review. Check the scope before drawing conclusions from what you find.

h3: Examine the sequence

p: Use the chronology to compare dates and follow the underlying records. The team assesses what an event establishes and how it affects the claim.

h3: Select the supporting records

p: Choose the material needed for the next stage of the work. Review the selected sources and the contents of any download before sharing them.
