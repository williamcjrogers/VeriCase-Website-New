# Source and capability review

10 October 2026. Scope: the requested unused-data callout and automated-bundle emphasis. Current primary sources were checked independently by the source-review agent and the parent agent. This record supersedes the earlier exclusion decision for the 95% claim following the owner's renewed request, but retains its evidential qualifications.

## Unused construction data

1. [Autodesk, Democratizing AECO data, 24 June 2024](https://adsknews.autodesk.com/en/news/democratizing-aeco-data/) publishes the over-95% unused-data statement for design and construction. Footnote 1 cites FMI (2018). Its separate footnote 2 concerns the joint Autodesk/FMI 2021 bad-data study. Those are different sources.
2. [FMI, Big Data white paper, 2018, page 2](https://fmicorp.com/uploads/media/FMI_BigDataReport.pdf#page=2) states 95.5% unused captured engineering and construction data, with 96% in its rounded graphic. It cites Brian Hill's 2017 Xpera article. The report covers a broader data universe than emails, documents or dispute evidence, including sensors, design and financial data.
3. [Brian Hill, Xpera, 31 October 2017, republished by Vertex](https://vertexeng.com/insights/digging-for-the-bigdata-gold-in-todays-construction-projects/) reports a 2016 keynote and a less-than-0.5% analysed-data figure. This is a different percentage and a different concept from unused data. No sampling or calculation method substantiating the later 95.5% figure was established in this source chain.

### Publication decision

Use **95%+**, “of design and construction data reported as unused”, and the immediately visible attribution **Autodesk, 24 June 2024, citing FMI (2018)**. Reference [4] explains the chain and discrepancy and links all three sources. This accurately identifies a published industry claim without labelling it a verified result from the joint Autodesk/FMI study.

Do not say 95% wasted, 95% of dispute evidence, 95% of documents, a recovery rate, an avoidable-dispute rate or VeriCase savings. Do not add competitor claims. Keep the three existing, separately qualified dispute figures and their original citation numbers.
