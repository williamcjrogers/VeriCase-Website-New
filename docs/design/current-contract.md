## Product presentation

The [product reference register](product-reference-register.md) governs what the three workspace illustrations may depict: a document read beside its file record (PR-03), a matching passage beside the document it comes from (PR-02) and a report exported with its structure and source links (PR-04). On 05 October 2026 these illustrations replaced the three historical application captures, whose QA fixtures, test text, typefaces and colours did not belong on the page; the reviewed originals are kept as evidence in `docs/design/product-references/` and no longer ship. Each illustration shows only the relationship the register records for its capture, with no controls, browser chrome, counts or processing states, and names no file format. The report is an output, not a drafting editor or proof of a completed claim.

Eleven labelled illustrations of one fictional matter stand on the page: the document beside its file record (the opening), a matching passage and the document it comes from, “From documents to chronology.”, Executive Analysis (“A question answered, then the next one.”), Deep Research (“A full report, and the bundle built from it.”), an opposing assertion tested against the record, “An argument with its sources.”, a claim section built from its records, a report exported with its structure and sources, a discussion kept with the record, and “One record, discussed in lanes.”. The matter (owner, 06 October 2026: “make it varied”) is a nine-storey residential building with a ground-floor nursery under the JCT Design and Build Contract 2016 with the Employer’s amendments, drawn from the patterns of real adjudications with nothing that could identify one: no names, places, sums or wording. Each illustration takes a different strand of it (a late answer to a request for information, a façade change recorded as having no time impact, a road approval, early access to the nursery, a reduced loading bay, landscaping revisions, who held the road works before contract, the security works, the forecast completion date, and an email the Contractor says was notice), and the page acts for the Contractor throughout. They depict outcomes, not application screens: fictional records (`MATTER_RECORDS` and each illustration’s own `sources` in `frontend/src/content/marketing.js`), set in the page’s existing type roles, cited by document and date as the exported report cites a record, with no application controls, reference numbers, counts or processing states. Each carries an “Illustration” label and an illustrative caption and the footer states that illustrations use a fictional matter. Since the owner’s instruction of 05 October 2026 every illustration is live and stands on an ink panel (the header’s green, with a brass top rule): the fictional records lie on it as paper, the type is parchment and brass, and each figure plays one operation once when it comes into view. Where the operation begins with something the user writes (a question and its follow-up, an opposing assertion, a section heading, a comment, a find term, a search), that text is typed in front of the reader and the answer then appears in turn; the chronology, the argument, the report and the lanes have no typed input and their answer appears in turn. A “Play again” control in each caption replays the performance; its place is held while the figure plays, so the caption does not move when it appears. No frame shows a spinner, a progress state, a count, a timing or a result the records do not contain; the typed line is complete for assistive technology from the start and holds its finished size, so nothing around it moves as it is typed; reduced motion, print and pages without the script show the end state, so nothing essential depends on the motion. The opening illustration carries four marginal notes in brass italic (the record selected, its file record, its original page, the passage found in the document), each joined by a hairline to the part it names: the notes and their leaders are brass-400 on the ink, and each leader turns brass-700 where it crosses the paper; on phones each note stands on the paper beneath its part, in brass-700. The reader’s views are named as the register records them and set as type, not tabs. No generated application screen, invented progress sequence or video may imply unsupported behaviour.

The [capability register](website-capability-register.md) records source support and limits. Repository support and a historical capture do not establish current customer availability. No unsupported speed, winning, truth-reconstruction, immutable-storage or automatic legal-outcome claim is permitted.
