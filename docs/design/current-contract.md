## Identity and composition

Retain the genuine VeriCase wordmark, forest green, paper, parchment and brass, self-hosted Newsreader and IBM Plex. The favicon (the wordmark’s V on the header’s ink green) and the app icons (the V | C monogram) are generated from the wordmark by `frontend/scripts/render-icons.mjs`; the sharing card `og-card.png` is rendered from `frontend/og/og.html` by `frontend/scripts/render-og.mjs` and shows the opening copy, not a product screen. The opening establishes construction claims and commercial teams, followed by a wide illustration of a document read beside its file record, drawn in the page’s own type and colours as a figure in an expert’s report would be, with marginal notes naming each part. Three practical jobs lead to substantive preparation, chronology, research, rebuttal, drafting, collaboration and source-review explanations.

Opening copy: **Evidence in order. Arguments on the record.** (approved by the owner on 05 October 2026, replacing “Complex evidence. Compelling arguments.”, whose second half described an effect on the reader rather than the method). The category is construction claims and disputes. The supporting sentence is: “Investigate the record, test competing accounts and develop your claim or response, with the evidence beside you.” Keep one demonstration action and concise email guidance. Audience detail belongs with collaboration and practitioner proof with the team introduction. On phones the opening uses a compact monochrome composition; desktop retains its existing editorial emphasis. Use green for principal headings, dark bronze for small action text on light grounds and pale text only over a verified dark surface. Keep readable prose, distinct section spacing and primary/standalone controls at least 44 pixels high.

The copy sells evidence investigation and case preparation through concrete tasks and benefits. The timing section begins **The project took years. Your response cannot.** Treat it as a recognisable dispute scenario, not a processing benchmark. Explain professional judgement once in the main source-review section and where needed in the FAQ. Preserve specific scope limitations, illustration labels and confidentiality guidance. The user describes VeriCase as a disruptor; convey the change in working method rather than adding unsupported superiority claims.

## Product presentation

The [product reference register](product-reference-register.md) governs what the three workspace illustrations may depict: a document read beside its file record (PR-03), a matching passage beside the document it comes from (PR-02) and a report exported with its structure and source links (PR-04). On 05 October 2026 these illustrations replaced the three historical application captures, whose QA fixtures, test text, typefaces and colours did not belong on the page; the reviewed originals are kept as evidence in `docs/design/product-references/` and no longer ship. Each illustration shows only the relationship the register records for its capture, with no controls, browser chrome, counts or processing states, and names no file format. The report is an output, not a drafting editor or proof of a completed claim.

Nine labelled illustrations of one fictional matter stand on the page: the document beside its file record (the opening), a matching passage and the document it comes from, “From documents to chronology.”, a question traced to its sources, an opposing assertion tested against the record, “An argument with its sources.”, a claim section built from its records, a report exported with its structure and sources, and a discussion kept with the record. They depict outcomes, not application screens: the same fictional records (`MATTER_RECORDS` and `EVIDENCE_ILLUSTRATION` in `frontend/src/content/marketing.js`), set in the page’s existing type roles, cited by document and date as the exported report cites a record, with no application controls, reference numbers, counts or processing states. Each carries an “Illustration” label and an illustrative caption, sits in a parchment frame with a brass top rule and no toolbar, and the footer states that illustrations use a fictional matter. The opening illustration carries four marginal notes in brass italic (the record selected, its file record, its original page, the passage found in the document), each joined by a hairline to the part it names; on phones each note stands beneath its part. The reader’s views are named as the register records them and set as type, not tabs. No generated application screen, invented progress sequence or video may imply unsupported behaviour.

The [capability register](website-capability-register.md) records source support and limits. Repository support and a historical capture do not establish current customer availability. No unsupported speed, winning, truth-reconstruction, immutable-storage or automatic legal-outcome claim is permitted.
