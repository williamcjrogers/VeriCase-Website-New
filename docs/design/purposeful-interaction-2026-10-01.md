## Approaches considered

1. Restore the original interactive walkthrough. Rejected: it restores depth but also the controls and visual complexity the owner disliked.
2. Keep every illustration static and add text. Rejected: it repeats the most recent over-correction and loses the useful connection between a statement and its evidence.
3. Restore full explanations and add direct source inspection. Recommended: familiar reading first, optional evidence inspection second, one action and one obvious result.

## Interaction contract

Illustrations are explicitly welcome when they explain something clearly. This is not a prescription to turn the website into text and disclosure controls. Review each visual individually: retain, simplify or remove according to what a first-time reader can understand. An ordered chronology and an argument connected to its evidence are useful illustrations in their own right, before anyone interacts.

- The default chronology states the date, event and outcome in plain language.
- Each source control explicitly says what opens, for example “Read the instruction”. Do not make an unexplained evidence ID the only affordance.
- Selection displays the existing exact excerpt, its document name and date in the same area. The selected state and return action are visible. No modal stack or multi-step operation.
- An argument citation can emphasise or reveal its associated source. It does not generate a new answer or simulate AI processing.
- Full product explanations and biographies remain readable without clicking. Questions and supplementary credentials can use native disclosure controls.
- Pointer, keyboard and touch must produce the same result; reduced motion removes movement without removing information.

## Review against the brief

Do not replace the current figure with another row of feature cards. Remove the redundant icon boxes and let the actual dated record carry the visual identity. Do not restore complex networks, scanning or invented performance counters. Do not rewrite approved copy to make an attractive layout easier. The distinctive element is the visible relationship between a statement and its source, and the rest of the page should support reading.

## Acceptance after design review

Compare approved passages word for word; inventory original capability paragraphs against rendered content; check all four biographies and roles; verify each interaction's default, selected, close and keyboard states; test 320, 390, 768 and 1440px without overflow. Preserve consent, routing and removal fixes. Only then run the full existing checks and publish the reviewed revision.
